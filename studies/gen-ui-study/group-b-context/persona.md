# Who this product is for

The product is a finance planning and reporting platform. People use the **Workflows** area to organize finance processes into workflows made of tasks, assign owners, and track progress until each one is complete.

Two people use these screens most.

---

## Ava, VP of Finance (primary)

**Role:** Runs planning and reporting for a 100-person company with a finance team of three. Has a background in public accounting, then corporate finance. Works in spreadsheets, accounting software and slide decks.

**What she needs**
- To see the state of each process at a glance, and know who owns what.
- To deliver accurate, timely, reliable reports.
- To spend less time on manual, repetitive tasks and more on strategy.
- To keep recurring cycles, like the monthly close, under control.

**What frustrates her**
- Chasing colleagues for numbers every month.
- Tedious manual work.
- The chaos of closing periods.
- Fear that something breaks without her noticing.

**How she works:** Learns by doing, not by reading manuals. Wants a tool that needs no training.

---

## Tobias, Director of Finance (secondary)

**Role:** Leads a finance team at a 500-person retail company. Has worked in the same finance organization for five years and knows the business well. Uses enterprise systems every day.

**What he needs**
- To keep a nimble team that responds quickly to requests.
- Clear accountability across the team and other departments.
- Confidence about versions and status when several people work on the same process.
- To present clear, compelling updates to executives.

**What frustrates him**
- Not knowing which version is current.
- His team spending too much time on manual tasks.
- Poor communication between departments.

**How he works:** Reviews many workflows and tasks at once, moves quickly between them, and expects dense data to stay readable.

---

## What this means for the design

These points come from the needs and frustrations above.

- **Status and ownership come first.** People look for who owns a process and where it stands before anything else.
- **Fewer clicks.** Repeated actions should be quick and predictable.
- **Clear, not clever.** Plain labels and familiar patterns. Nobody should need a manual.
- **Trust.** The screen should make it obvious what is being shown, so users never wonder whether they are looking at the right data.

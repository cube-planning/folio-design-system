Chart form — graphic treatment

Marketing / graphic-design treatment: shape and color carry the story, not the numbers. Specimen for glass-ui-style.md § 4 and the form rules in the brand README. Not the product/report treatment — see the note at the foot of the page.

1 — Three-series categorical, over glass
Revenue by segment
FY26 · $M · actuals through Jun
✓Slots 1 / 4 / 6 — teal, indigo, orange. Slot 2 (cyan) sits next to teal on the wheel, so the glass recipe skips it for a three-series graphic.
✓No per-point numbers — a value beside every dot competes with the line it belongs to. The shape of the trend is the message here; the y-axis carries magnitude.
✓8px borderless bullets (r=4) — the marker floor, and ~4× the stroke width. Past ~10px the dots start dominating the line they sit on.
✓2px stroke, round caps, no area fill. Two cools + one warm accent — a near-triadic split, so the three read as a set rather than three unrelated hues.
2 — Actuals vs forecast, and variance
Total revenue
Actuals to Apr, forecast to Jun · $M
✓Same hue, forecast dashed 4 4 — the hue is not switched for forecast, so the series still reads as one measure.
Budget variance
Favorable vs unfavorable · $K
✓Diverging pair, not red/green — warm orange vs cool indigo across a neutral gray-500 zero. Red/green separate by only ΔE 8.0 under deuteranopia; this pair scores 32.5.
✓Two channels, not one — the poles differ in temperature and lightness, so the split survives greyscale printing. Sign is carried by the column's side of the zero line, so it never rests on hue alone.
✓4px radius on the value end only — the baseline end stays square, so the bar reads as anchored.
Scope — read before reusing this

These are decorative graphics, not readable charts. No value on this page can be read to a number, by design — the trend shape and the color relationships are the whole payload.

That makes this the graphic-design / marketing treatment only: hero images, section illustrations, deck covers, anywhere a chart is standing in for "we do finance analytics" rather than reporting a figure.

Product and report charts are the opposite case and are governed by docs/BRAND-FOUNDATION.md § "Chart data-point display": every plotted value must be readable without interaction, because the reader is there for the number and will screenshot it into a board deck where hover does not exist. Do not copy this page's treatment into a dashboard, a report, or anything a persona reads figures off.
